01 — The company
1998

A trading house built for project supplies.

ES Electrical Switchgears & Controls Pvt Ltd (ESCPL) is a professional trading company, established in 1998 in the south part of Delhi, with the primary objective of trading activities of electrical products in the Delhi/NCR region.

We service both domestic and project customers with a team of well-qualified members, and are now a company with a ₹200 Cr annual turnover across four locations. ESCPL keeps pace with new trading technologies and marketing demands, identify the customer, satisfy the customer, keep the customer, and above all keep the customer happy.

In our business, customer satisfaction is the primary goal to achieve the company's growth. We strive to achieve the motto of HAPPY CUSTOMER.
